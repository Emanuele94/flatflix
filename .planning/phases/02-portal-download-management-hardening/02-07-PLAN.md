<objective>
Let the household cancel a download from the list and have it really leave qBittorrent: an in-progress download is deleted with its partial files, a completed one is only removed from the list and keeps the film on disk. The browser sends an opaque id and nothing else; the server re-reads the torrent, re-checks the category and decides on its own whether files go. The job behind a Preparing download is stopped first, so a cancel never comes back minutes later as a "Not available" row and never blocks a fresh tap.

Purpose: DOWN-05 (cancel from the list, removed from qBittorrent) and DOWN-06 for cancel (502 codes, never a 401). Decisions D-32, D-33 (rated reversible in CONTEXT), D-34, D-35.
Output: DELETE route, `DownloadPipeline.cancel`, `JobRegistry.cancel_for`, the files decision in listing.py and the list's `action` derived from it, tests.
</objective>
