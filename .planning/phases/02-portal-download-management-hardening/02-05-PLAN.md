<objective>
Throttle the two routes that start work in qBittorrent: at most 10 searches and 20 download starts per minute from one address, with a 429 `too_many_requests` the frontend turns into "Too many requests. Wait a moment and try again." The limits are generous on purpose (D-38): only a stuck finger or a script should hit them. The sliding-window limiter from the login route is reused, extracted into its own module so its name fits every use.

Purpose: SEC-05 (search and download endpoints rate limited with a clear message), decisions D-38 (limits, env-configurable, keying per address) and D-39 (the 429 shape the UI maps; the text itself is plan 02-08).
Output: app/throttle.py, two settings, two throttled handlers, test_rate_limit.py.
</objective>

## Flagged assumptions

- When the host hides real client addresses (Docker Desktop and WSL2), every phone shares one key, so the limits become household-wide (same caveat as the login lockout); 10 searches and 20 downloads per minute stay generous for a household. Plan 02-11 documents it in docs/setup.md.
- Stale per-address keys are pruned only when that address is seen again; with a handful of household addresses this is negligible (RESEARCH Pattern 4).
