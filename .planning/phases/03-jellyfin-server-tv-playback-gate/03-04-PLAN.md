## Flagged assumptions

- D-84 "one per codec combination" is implemented as the full video x audio x container cross product (24 gating rows) plus one-factor subtitle rows; encoding each video and audio stream once and muxing by copy keeps generation to minutes. RESEARCH proposed a smaller one-factor set; the cross product follows the decision text.
- If ffmpeg's MP4 muxer refuses DTS, the MP4 DTS rows read `n/a — <reason>` and do not gate; the SUMMARY names them.
- The exact Jellyfin test-video file names are chosen by the executor from the live directory listing and pinned with their published sha256; RESEARCH verified the listing and the sidecars, not specific names.
- Jellyfin may clean codec words (h264, hevc, aac, dts, ac3) out of a home-video item's name; the row id at the start survives, so the Compat check and the plan 03-06 helper match on the first token only.
