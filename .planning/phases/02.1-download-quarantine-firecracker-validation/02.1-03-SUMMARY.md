# Phase 02.1 Plan 03: Backend Rules, Runner Client and Subtitle Check Summary

**A typed runner client (host faults mean wait, refusals mean unprobeable), a pure judge that applies the QUAR-03 rules to the runner's facts, the choice of which torrent files to verify, and a VM-free subtitle check, all tested without KVM.**

- **Completed:** 2026-10-23T19:26:14Z

## Accomplishments

- `RunnerClient.probe/health/selftest/aclose`; `ProbeFacts.from_json` accepts only the contract shape and raises ValueError otherwise (T-02.1-10).
- `judge` passes only on status ok plus container family, a real non-cover video stream, a finite positive duration, the minimum duration and the bitrate band; the size is the backend's own. The research fixtures reach their decided verdicts.
- `files_to_check` keeps finished, selected, non-hidden, non-sample video and subtitle files; a film's largest video (lowest index on a tie) is main, series videos are all main. `min_minutes_for` gives extras no minimum.
- `fx-ok-<epoch>` tag helpers and a strict `in_quarantine`.
- `check_subtitle`: size caps before content, NUL and control-byte screening, UTF-16 BOM decoding, bitmap subtitles on a size cap only.
- `FakeRunner` and the fact builders are ready for plans 05 to 09.
