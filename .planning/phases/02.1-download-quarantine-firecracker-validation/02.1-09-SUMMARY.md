## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] A proxy-name lookup could hold every request for about 5 s and deadlock the stack start**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (live `docker compose up -d --build --wait`: backend reported unhealthy, `web` stayed in Created)
- **Issue:** `TrustedProxyHeaders` resolves `TRUSTED_PROXY_HOSTS=web` per request when the peer is not trusted and the last lookup is older than 2 s. While the `web` container was not started, Docker's embedded DNS forwarded the name upstream and failed only after about 5 s, so requests, including the container healthcheck (3 s timeout), stalled 3.6-5.9 s. The backend never became healthy, so Compose never started `web`, which kept the name unresolvable. The event loop was not blocked (the main thread sat in `epoll_wait` for 60 of 60 samples during a stall; a thread-pool thread sat in `poll` with a 5 s timeout, the resolver). A first hypothesis of a blocking verifier tick was therefore wrong and nothing was moved to `asyncio.to_thread`.
- **Fix:** the lookup is capped at `RESOLVE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` (1.0 s) with `asyncio.wait_for`, and refreshes are serialised by a lock with a re-check, so concurrent requests share one lookup. An unresolved name still trusts nobody.
- **Tests:** `test_a_name_that_never_resolves_cannot_hold_a_request_for_long`, `test_concurrent_requests_share_one_lookup` (red first, then green).
- **Files modified:** backend/app/proxy.py, backend/tests/test_proxy.py
- **Commit:** `bbe92c3`
- **Verified live:** after the rebuild all four services are healthy and `curl http://127.0.0.1/` answers 200.

**2. [Note, not a defect] Unauthenticated `GET /api/downloads` polling seen by the orchestrator**
- It came from this executor's own timing probes while diagnosing item 1 (a few dozen requests), not from smoke.py.
