<objective>
Finish the decision path for files that do not pass: retry a crashed or timed-out probe once, hold failing files owner-only in `rejected/`, remove the torrent at once so a suspect file is never seeded, keep a small outcome record so the household still sees the result after the torrent is gone, write the admin's audit line, and purge both after seven days. Mixed packs deliver what passed. A link that cannot be made never turns into a copy.

Purpose: QUAR-02 (crash/timeout counts as failure after one retry), QUAR-03 (failing files never pass), QUAR-04 (failing files never reach media, torrent removed, reason logged). Decisions D-47, D-48, D-55, D-57, D-60, D-61, D-62, D-77.
Output: `OutcomeStore`, the rejected hand-off, the collision policy, the purge, four audit events, and their tests.
</objective>
