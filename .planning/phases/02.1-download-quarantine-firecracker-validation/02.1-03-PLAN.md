## Flagged assumptions

- The verified marker is a qBittorrent tag `fx-ok-<epoch>` (CONTEXT left the choice to Claude, D-54). Torrents carrying it leave qBittorrent within the D-21 share limits, so a later change of the tag format affects at most a day of torrents: rated reversible.

- A file the runner refuses (400/404/409) is treated by the verifier as unprobeable (a failure), never as a pass.
